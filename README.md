## Background: 
Socio-economic indicators are statistical measures that describe the economic and social conditions of a population or region. 
They help assess well-being, development, and inequality. Common indicators include:

- Economic Indicators: 
GDP per capita, unemployment rate, income distribution, and poverty rate.
- Social Indicators: 
Education levels, literacy rate, life expectancy, healthcare access, and housing conditions.

- Composite Indicators: 
Human Development Index (HDI) and Gini coefficient.
These indicators are used by governments, researchers, and organizations to guide policy-making and track progress over time. 

## Objectives: 
In this work package we would like to focus on socio-economic indicators related to living conditions, and measures on equality or inequality in the population. Socio-economic indicators are made and requested at a lot of different regional or administrative levels. 
The work package will make an inventory of existing indicators from, nationally (Country, County and municipalities) and internationally (Eurostat, OECD, UN) levels.
After making an inventory of indicators we would further make an assessment of feasibilities and relevance of making indicators on local administrative unit level (LAU, municipalities) and upwards. The assessment will include different aspects such as e.g. availability of data, quality of data, ease and cost of data capture and analysis etc.
We believe that breaking indicators down to LAU level will increase the usefulness of indicators related to different EU policies, as indicators at national level and above often masks regional differences.
The work package will also investigate feasibility of different methods. 

## Expected outcomes and deliveries: 
Expected outcomes are inventories of existing socio-economic indicators at different geographical levels and likewise inventories of available sources of statistics with quality assessments. These outcomes will be parts of deliveries of this work package. Where relevant, outcomes from other work packages will be included into this overarching work package.
